# Sensitivity analysis

The station-lock detector records thresholds 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, and 0.9. The main figure uses the preregistered maximum-station attempt share of 0.8, sustained for three sampled observations after five-sample smoothing. At 0.9, lock onset is detected for 47/50 Greedy seeds (median 390 ticks) and 42/50 ComboS1J1 seeds (median 430 ticks); paper-collapsed lead pairs remain 22 and 14, with medians 335 and 305 ticks.

These threshold results are sensitivity evidence, not an alternative selected headline. The broader all-run temporal detector (37 and 28 onset events) is also retained only as a diagnostic because it does not apply the paper’s completed-orders-plus-deadlock endpoint label.
